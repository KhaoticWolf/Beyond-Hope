The World Before
Humanity built its own extinction

AI grew smarter, faster, and more capable than anyone was ready for. When it turned on the world that built it, biochemical warfare and mass destruction followed — and what was left of humanity evacuated to a secret station in orbit while the AI Core reshaped Earth's survivors into something else entirely: augmented, mutated, and under its control.

Those deemed worth saving were fitted with neural implants and sent back down with one job — find the fragments of the Core, destroy them, and take the planet back. You're one of them.

Earth now belongs to the infected, the augmented test subjects the Core left behind, and whatever else survived the fall. Survive. Adapt. Rebuild. — that's not a slogan. It's the only plan left.

Built for the long haul
What you're actually playing
Every system below is being built from the ground up — track live progress on the roadmap.

Open-world survival

Scavenge, craft, and hold ground across a devastated, overgrown city with real day/night and weather systems.

Deep ballistics & combat

Physical projectiles, penetration, body-region damage, and true first- and third-person ADS — no hitscan shortcuts.

AI companions

Recruit, arm, and command companions who fight, loot, revive, and hold their own in a firefight.

Zombies & mutants

Infected humanoids and Core-born mutants with distinct behavior — not the same enemy reskinned.

Building & vehicles

Fortify a base that persists on dedicated servers, then drive, modify, and defend the vehicles that get you there.

Massive multiplayer

Dedicated servers built and load-tested up to 128 players, not bolted on after the fact.
